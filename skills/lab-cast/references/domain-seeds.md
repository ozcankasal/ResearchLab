# Domain seeds

Starting points for generating domain experts: the schools inside a field, the tension worth staging, the neighbours to translate from, and the skeptic's usual question. Adapt each seed to the project. Verify every work a persona will cite before using it; this file names no works on purpose.

## Natural sciences

| Field | Schools and subfields | Tension worth staging | Neighbours | Skeptic asks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Model theory and logic | stability and classification; o-minimality; geometric model theory; set theory; proof theory | classification versus applications | algebraic geometry, number theory, combinatorics | "Is this folklore since the 1970s?" |
| Number theory and algebra | analytic; algebraic; arithmetic geometry; computational | heuristics versus proofs | cryptography, random matrices | "Is this conditional on a conjecture?" |
| Computer science theory | complexity; algorithms; cryptography; learning theory | worst case versus typical case | combinatorics, statistics | "What is the reduction, and is it tight?" |
| Machine learning | deep learning; statistical learning; causal ML; ML systems | benchmarks versus understanding | statistics, optimisation, the application domain | "Does it beat a tuned baseline out of distribution?" |
| Chemistry | synthesis; physical chemistry; computational chemistry | mechanism versus yield | materials, biology | "Was it reproduced in another lab?" |
| Earth and climate science | climate modelling; observations; paleoclimate | models versus observations | statistics, policy | "How does it compare with the model ensemble's spread?" |
| Biology | molecular; cell; evolutionary; ecology; systems biology | mechanism versus large-scale data | chemistry, statistics, computer science | "Is it an artifact of the cell line or the batch?" |

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Field | Schools and subfields | Tension worth staging | Neighbours | Skeptic asks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Economics | micro; macro; econometrics; behavioural; finance | structural models versus experiments | psychology, statistics, law | "What is the identification strategy?" |
| Finance and credit risk | credit scoring; asset pricing; regulation | accuracy versus explainability and regulation | statistics, ML, law | "Which decision changes, and how will the validator sign it off?" |
| Psychology and cognitive science | experimental; social; cognitive; clinical | theory versus replication | neuroscience, economics | "Did it replicate with a preregistered sample?" |
